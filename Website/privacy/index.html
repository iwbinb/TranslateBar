Privacy

Your words stay yours.

TranslateBar is designed to process translation content on your Mac.

At a glance

Effective July 21, 2026

  • TranslateBar does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.
  • TranslateBar includes no analytics, advertising, tracking, or tracking domains.
  • Preferences are stored locally on your Mac.

Translation

Translation is performed by translation technology built into macOS. TranslateBar does not send translation text to its own servers or to third-party translation services.

macOS may download language resources needed by its translation and speech technologies. Those system services are governed by the applicable system privacy terms and your device settings.

Optional features

Optional clipboard translation reads copied text only while the feature is enabled and processes it locally.

Optional dictation uses on-device speech recognition provided by macOS. TranslateBar does not retain microphone audio.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit TranslateBar Support or email hello@arenovo.com.
